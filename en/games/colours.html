The Colour Lab

How to play
  1. Tap up to 3 colours to put them on the palette (red, yellow, blue, white, black).
  2. Press MIX: the new colour appears with its name.
  3. Clear to start again and try another mix.
  4. Then make that colour with real paints and give it a funny name, like "Grumpy Frog Green"!
