Murphy's Wheel

How to play
  1. A child taps SPIN: the wheel stops on one of Murphy's 8 mishaps (the missing sock, the keys in the fridge…).
  2. Put the phone down and act out that mishap for real, like a little sketch, in your silliest voice.
  3. At the end, all together: "…and it was Murphy!"
  4. Then someone else spins. It teaches kids to laugh at mishaps instead of getting angry. After 5 minutes the phone stops: keep playing for real.
Spin the wheel!
Now it's your turn: act out the mishap for real, in the silliest voice you have. And at the end: "...and it was Murphy!"
