The Solution Die

How to play
  1. When something goes wrong (a lost game, a homework mistake), tap ROLL THE DIE.
  2. The die picks a move to start again: three breaths, tell it like sports commentators, look for one good thing…
  3. Do the move right away, for real, all together.
  4. Didn't work? Roll again. Kids learn there is always a move to start again.
