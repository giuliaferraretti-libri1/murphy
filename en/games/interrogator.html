The Interrogator Game

How to play
  1. The grown-up types up to 5 questions about the paragraph the child just read (homework or a book).
  2. Tap START THE INTERROGATION and read the questions in your detective voice.
  3. For each answer tap Right answer or Needs review.
  4. At the end the questions to review appear: write them on index cards and put them in the AGAIN box.

For the grown-up: type up to 5 questions about the paragraph your child just read.
