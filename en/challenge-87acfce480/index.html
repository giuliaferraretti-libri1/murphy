On the road 🚗

Games for car trips: the phone stays with a passenger, everybody plays by looking out of the window. The driver only plays with their voice!
How to play
  1. One passenger holds the phone. Everybody looks out of the window.
  2. When someone spots a thing on the card (a cow, a bridge, a red car…), they shout its name.
  3. Tap that square: it turns green.
  4. Get 4 in a row (across, down or diagonal) and shout BINGO! Confetti!
  5. Tap New card to play again with different things. Nobody spotted the cow? Murphy hid it. Next card!
How to play
  1. Start with the letter A (the one that blinks).
  2. Look for it on road signs, trucks, shops and number plates.
  3. The first person who sees it shouts “A!” and shows where it is.
  4. Tap Found it!: now look for B, then C… all the way to Z.
  5. Play as a team, or keep score of who finds the most letters. Your progress is saved for the next trip. Restart begins again from A.

  1. Tap New colour: the phone picks a colour at random (or a player calls one out).

Murphy's Science Quiz 🔬

How to play
  1. Read the question out loud (a grown-up can read it to younger kids).
  2. Tap the answer you think is right.
  3. Green = correct! Red = oops: read the “did you know” fact, now you know it!
  4. 10 questions per game. At the end you see your score.
  5. Tap Play again for 10 new questions. Play as a family: who gets the best score? Whoever loses plays again!

Colour Murphy 🖍️

How to colour
  1. Tap a drawing to open it (perfect for car trips!).
  2. Pick a colour at the bottom, then tap a white area: it fills up with colour. Use the Brush to paint freely with your finger.
  3. Made a mistake? Tap Undo. Blame Murphy and try again!
  4. Your colours are saved by themselves: close it and finish later.
  5. Tap Save to keep your finished picture on the phone. Clear starts again from white.
